Home/Exteriors

Exterior carpentry

Everything outside the walls — trim, siding, decks, porches and the structure behind them. This is the part of a New Jersey house that takes the beating, and it's where small problems get expensive fastest.

Why exteriors fail here

It's a freeze and thaw problem

Northern New Jersey runs through dozens of freeze-thaw cycles a year. Water finds a seam in the fall, freezes, expands, and opens that seam a little wider. Next season it gets in further.

That's why exterior damage here is almost never where you first see it. By the time paint is bubbling on a fascia board, water has usually been getting behind it for two or three winters, and it has been travelling — into the sheathing, along the rafter tails, down inside the wall cavity.

So we assess exteriors as a system rather than as a list of boards. Where is the water getting in, where is it going, and what has it already reached? Replace the board without answering those and you've bought yourself about five years.

Every exterior repair is really a water question. The carpentry is just the answer.

Services

What we handle outside

Three service lines, though most jobs cross between them. A siding repair frequently turns into trim work; a deck rebuild frequently turns into structural repair at the ledger.

Rot & trim repair

Fascia, soffit, corner boards, window and door casing, rake and frieze boards, porch ceilings and column wraps. Rebuilt in cellular PVC so it's finished for good.

  • Free written assessment
  • Structural repair included

Siding

Repair, partial replacement and full re-side. Cedar, fiber cement, engineered wood and vinyl — plus the water barrier and flashing details behind them that actually do the work.

  • Matching & blending
  • Full envelope detailing

Decks & porches

New builds, rebuilds and structural repair. Footings, ledger attachment and flashing done properly, because that's where old decks fail and where inspectors look first.

  • Permit & code compliant
  • Composite or wood

Assessment

What we check

Every exterior assessment covers the same list, on every elevation of the house, whether or not you asked about it.

  • Roof edge — fascia, soffit, rake boards, rafter tail condition, gutter sizing and pitch
  • Wall field — siding condition, fastener staining, buckling, gaps at butt joints
  • Penetrations — windows, doors, lights, hose bibs, vents, anywhere the envelope is cut
  • Terminations — corner boards, water table, band boards, where siding meets grade or roof
  • Attachments — decks, porches, railings, columns, and how they're tied to the house
  • Ground contact — grade clearance, splash-back, sill and rim joist condition where we can reach it

Anything soft gets probed. You get the findings in writing with photos, sorted by whether it needs doing now, soon, or not yet.
