From here

01

We call you back

Usually the same day. A few questions to work out whether this is something we should look at in person, or something we can answer over the phone and save you the appointment.

02

We come out and assess

Free, and typically within the week — sooner if it's urgent. We walk the whole property, not just the one thing you wrote to us about, and we probe anything that looks suspect rather than guessing from the driveway.

03

You get it in writing

A condition report with photos, sorted by urgency: what needs doing now, what can wait, what's worth watching. Plus a line-itemed quote for anything you want us to take on.

You decide

No follow-up pressure and no call cycle. The report is yours to keep either way, including if you take it to another contractor.
