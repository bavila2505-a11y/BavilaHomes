Privacy policy

B. Avila Home Improvements (“Bavila Homes,” “we”) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why, and what we do with it. Last updated September 28, 2026.

This website

When you send a request through our contact form, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email, address and a description of the work. We use them only to respond to you, schedule an assessment and prepare a quote.

The site uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors find and use it. These services may set cookies and collect standard information such as pages viewed, device and browser type and approximate location. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with an ad blocker.

Our contact form is processed by our website host, Netlify, on our behalf.

Our business systems

We use internal software, including an application called “Bavila Agents,” to run our own business. With our authorization, it connects to our company Google account (Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar) to organize project files, summarize and draft email, and keep job schedules.

  • It accesses only Google accounts owned by Bavila Homes. It is not offered to the public or to customers.
  • Google user data is used only to provide these internal features. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties, except the service providers that process it for us (such as our AI provider) under confidentiality terms.
  • Data is stored on systems we control and protected with access controls. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account’s security settings.
  • Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing, retention and your choices

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the people and services needed to do your job, such as subcontractors, suppliers, permitting offices and our accountant, or when the law requires it.

We keep customer and project records for as long as needed for the work, warranties, and tax and legal obligations.

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, call (201) 421-0080 or write to us at 685 Lincoln Blvd, Westwood, NJ 07675.
