# Photos — currently switched off

The site is running photo-free on purpose. Nothing is broken and nothing is missing.

## Turning photos on later

1. Put your images in this folder using the names below
2. Open `_source/pages.py`, find `SHOW_PHOTOS = False` near the top, change it to `True`
3. Run `python3 build.py`

Every photo slot switches on across the site. If you'd rather not run the script, say the word and I'll generate the photo version for you to swap in.

## Getting shots worth using

You don't need a good camera. A recent phone is fine. What you need is discipline:

- **Mark where you stood.** Chalk mark, paver, fence post — anything. A before/after pair from two different angles proves nothing. From the identical angle it's undeniable, and it's the single thing that separates a real portfolio from stock.
- **Overcast beats sunny.** Direct sun blows out white trim and buries everything else in shadow.
- **Shoot the after before you pack up.** This is the one everybody forgets, and you can't go back for it.
- **Landscape orientation.** The slots crop to 4:3.
- **Get the middle shot too** — the rot you cut out, the open wall, the flood-tested pan. It isn't used in these slots, but it's the most persuasive photo a framer can own. Use it in ads, on your Google Business Profile, and in assessment reports.

## Before uploading

Resize to roughly 1600px on the long edge, save around 80% JPEG quality. Raw phone photos are 4–8 MB each and will make the site slow. Free: squoosh.app, or Preview on Mac (Tools → Adjust Size).

## Rights

Only photographs of work your crew actually did. Anything else is a misrepresentation problem under New Jersey's home improvement advertising rules and a copyright problem on top of it.
